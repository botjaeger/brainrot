What it does

The popup has the switch, shows which feed you're on, and counts what you watched.

Moves on by itself

Uses each site's own next button, so the feed behaves as if you swiped.

Plays in the background

Switch tabs and the video keeps going. The feed moves on when you come back.

Pause means pause

Stop a video yourself and it stays stopped. One switch turns everything off.

Counts what you watched

Today and all-time, per site, kept only in your browser.

Questions

Does it work in background tabs?

Yes. Switch tabs and the video keeps playing. The feed moves on when you come back.

Is it free?

Yes, completely. No account needed.

Will it slow my browser down?

No. It only listens for the end of the video on feed pages and stays idle everywhere else.

Safari? Phones?

Safari is coming. Phones aren't supported: it's a desktop browser extension.

Can I see the code?

Yes. Brainrot is open source under the MIT license, on GitHub.

Privacy

The extension collects nothing. Settings and counts stay in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. No account.

When hosted on Vercel, this website counts anonymous page views with Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies.
