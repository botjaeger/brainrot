What it does

Moves on by itself Uses each site's own next button, so the feed behaves as if you swiped.
Plays in the background Switch tabs and the video keeps going. The feed moves on when you come back.
Pause means pause Stop a video yourself and it stays stopped. One switch turns everything off.
Counts what you watched Today and all-time, per site, kept only in your browser.

Questions

Does it work in background tabs?

Yes. Switch tabs and the video keeps playing. The feed moves on when you come back.

Is it free?

Yes, completely. No account needed.

Will it slow my browser down?

No. It only listens for the end of the video on feed pages and stays idle everywhere else.

Safari? Phones?

Safari is coming. Phones aren't supported: it's a desktop browser extension.

Privacy

The extension collects nothing. No personal data is collected, stored or sent anywhere; everything runs in your browser. No account.

When hosted on Vercel, this website counts anonymous page views with Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies.
